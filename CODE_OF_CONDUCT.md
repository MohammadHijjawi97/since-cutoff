# Code of conduct

since-cutoff follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, criticise ideas and code rather than
people, and remember that many contributors are new to open source or writing in a second
language. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour in a project space worries you, report it privately to the maintainer,
[@MohammadHijjawi97](https://github.com/MohammadHijjawi97), through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/MohammadHijjawi97/since-cutoff/security/advisories/new)
(choose it even though it says "vulnerability"; say in the title that it is a conduct report).
You can also use GitHub's own [report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
form for content on GitHub.

Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove
comments, lock threads or block accounts that break this code, following the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
